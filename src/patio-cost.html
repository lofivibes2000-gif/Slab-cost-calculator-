Patio specs

How to spec a patio that performs

The standard patio specification

  • 4 inches of concrete — the standard residential minimum, and enough for foot traffic, furniture, and a grill
  • 3,000–3,500 PSI concrete; 4,000 PSI with 5–7% air entrainment in freeze-thaw climates
  • 4 inches of compacted crushed stone base
  • Welded wire mesh or fiber reinforcement, at mid-depth
  • Control joints every 8–10 feet, sawn within a few hours of finishing
  • Curing compound applied as soon as the surface water is gone

The IRC does not regulate exterior patios. ACI 330R-08 states there is no benefit in building slabs less than 4 inches thick.

Where to go thicker

  • Hot tubs and outdoor kitchens — thicken to 6 inches under the concentrated load area with #4 rebar at 12 in on center
  • Heavy planters — 6 inches with #4 rebar
  • Freeze-thaw climates — stay at 4 inches but upgrade to 4,000 PSI with air entrainment; air matters more than thickness
