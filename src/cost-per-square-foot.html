Why prices differ

What pushes a quote up or down

Site access

If the truck can back up and chute the concrete in, there is no extra charge. If the pour is in a backyard, uphill, or past a fence, you need a pump — $450 to $900 minimum, rising with volume. Wheelbarrow-only access adds $1–$2 per sq ft in labor.

Ground condition

Poor soil needs engineering fill, extra compaction, and deeper excavation. This can add $1–$2.50 per sq ft, and it is the first place to look when a bid seems high.

Slab size

Small slabs carry more fixed cost per square foot. A 10 x 10 pad runs $8.50–$15.00 per sq ft; a 40 x 60 driveway runs $6.50–$12.50. The rate drops because delivery and crew setup spread out.

Removal and haul-off

Tearing out an old 4-inch slab runs about $4 per sq ft to demo and haul, more as it gets thicker. Often the second-biggest line item after labor.
