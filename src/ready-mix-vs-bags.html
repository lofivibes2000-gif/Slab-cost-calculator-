Ready-Mix vs Bags

Ready-mix or bags of concrete?

The breakeven sits at about 1 cubic yard — 45 bags of 80 lb mix. Below that, bags cost less. Above it, ready-mix wins on both money and time. This calculator prices your specific pour both ways so you can see which one it is.

$130–$180per yd³ ready-mix
$5.50–$7.75per 80 lb bag
~1 yd³breakeven point
The rule of thumb
Where each one wins
  • Under 1 yd³ — bags. A 10 x 10 patio at 4 inches. No delivery fee, no short-load fee, no minimum order.
  • 1 to 2 yd³ — either. Compare the delivered quote against bags plus a mixer rental.
  • Over 2 yd³ — ready-mix, with a pump if the truck cannot reach the forms.

Past 1 yd³, hand-mixing also risks cold joints between batches.

Why the totals look so close: bagged mix at $5.50–$7.75 per bag works out to $248–$349 per cubic yard, versus $145–$195 delivered. The bagged rate looks worse on paper — but it avoids the delivery trip fee and the short-load surcharge that make a small ready-mix order expensive.
