The most important detail
Support it at mid-depth

Rebar must sit about 2 inches off the bottom of a 4-inch slab, on wire chairs or concrete blocks (dobies).

Rebar resting flat on the sub-base adds almost nothing. It has to be inside the concrete matrix, in the upper third, where the tension actually develops. This is the single most common mistake on residential pours — and it is invisible once the concrete is down.
